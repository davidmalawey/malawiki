## Description

This video is an INVITATION for the open source community of makers to improve and play with the combination of PVC and ABS because these two polymers bond with immense strength using low-cost adhesive (found in the hardware store with PVC pipes).  My key audience is makers who build things and design things, often using CAD and 3D printing.  So if you are seeking entertainment you may be disappointed - that is not the purpose.

It's ALL OPEN SOURCE - that means the designs belong to the world.  The video is not about getting credit or impressing someone.  Most of my viewers don't often hang out in a room with two engineers and hands-on projects.  But they do spend time building things and exploring designs.   The reason I uploaded the video is to show there is plenty of space for innovation, there are plenty of ways to make useful things instead of figurines on the 3D printer, and my community is open to help and exchange ideas to help you succeed in building.

Follow the link below to download the CAD model & design parameters with all the maths. (math optional).
