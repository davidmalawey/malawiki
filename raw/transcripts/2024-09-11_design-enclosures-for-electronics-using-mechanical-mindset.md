## Description

Tips, ingredients, solutions, and steps on designing an enclosure for your electronics project.  This is for students or professionals in the prototyping stage, with experience taken from my mechanical design background as well as 5-10 years of senior design projects at Texas A&M.  

The goal for the scope was to inform on planning and considerations that are usually not found in design videos.  It is not a CAD modeling tutorial, or a 3D printing tutorial, althought it includes those topics.  Please share if this is helpful and which parts helped you!
