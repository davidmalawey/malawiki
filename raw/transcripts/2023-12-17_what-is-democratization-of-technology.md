## Description

Hello friends,

This video is my first effort to break down what it means to democratize a technology.  It also answers why it is helpful, why it matters, and what can be done if a technology is democratized.

It's far more powerful than offering something for free.  Free simply means there is no money required but democratization means you hand over the entire force of a technology to a population to meet their own needs with it.
