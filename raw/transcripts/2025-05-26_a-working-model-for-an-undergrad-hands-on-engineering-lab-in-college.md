## Description

This video is for Educators who want to build a multidisciplinary hands-on lab.  In 40 minutes I share some old photos of lab spaces & robotics activities from my work at Texas A&M.  I am treating this like a case study to tell the story of the structure, management of the students & other people involved.  I talk through how it is integrated in the regular curriculum, integrated with grad students occasionally, and outside businesses or sponsors who interact with the people in the lab.

Dr. Morgan called this the MISL lab, Multidisciplinary Integrated Systems Lab, and it operated I think from before 2012  up to 2021 when he retired.

My advisor from 2016, Dr. Joseph Morgan, had established a sustainable model for students to build robotics & develop experience beyond the classroom.   This answers how the students get paid, how equipment is handled, and how knowledge is disseminated, and building access takes place.  It tells how the students can be arranged to manage themselves, and trusted sufficiently to keep the work going and keep tools maintained in an academic environment and with reasonable budgets.
