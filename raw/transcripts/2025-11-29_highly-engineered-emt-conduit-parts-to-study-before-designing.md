## Description

Design engineers should be exposed to these OTS parts with magnificent design features.  EMT Conduit, electrical boxes and the parts which interface between them contain thousands of low cost solutions that easily integrate in your projects.  If you design 3D printed models, it's smart to study these first.

I personally studied each of these parts before making 3D models for OpenBox - the handy electrical box alone is a better solution for thousands of our 3D printed boxes out there, and beyond that we have an ecosystem of solutions that you can integrate in a system design.

Check out https://qr.net/openboxproject for designs relating to the handybox
And https://qr.net/openlabproject for the open source multidisciplinary engineering lab
