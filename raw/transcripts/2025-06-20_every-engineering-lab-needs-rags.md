## Description

In a previous video, I informed the audience that our Universities in Texas have fallen behind those in developing countries.   In this video I'm showing one set of instructions that directly informs how to meet expectations of engineering students at the bare minimum level.   This stuff is easy, but our priority must be the students and not the football stadiums.  Glorify learning, not fame.

In the video I described how the issue of missing rags cascades into poor outcomes for the students, poor confidence in job interviews, and so forth.   This is real life and the solutions are here so that we do not become a laughing stock among the world's engineers.

To Prospective students: This video shows you exactly what to look for when you tour a college.  This is how you know if the university is serious about building up great engineers or not.

To University administrators:  This is step-by-step instructions on how to support great engineering.  Overlooking the fundamental elements is impermissible.
