## Description

[summary]
as an engineering educator and former Toyota design engineer, I am sharing a rough outline of a process to produce designs & models that are truly competitive as designs with the broader options of designs for a given model. We’re not confined to open-source environments, rather opensource is the only way to guarantee a possible best-in-the-world result. 

I talk about the designers commitment to a long-term effort and how we can gain feedback from the best experts in the world on the journey to make a masterful design. I used the Lennox operating system as an example and breakdown why there is no operating system that can compete with it or come even remotely close.  The situation is based on the fact that a closed-box design can never be actually verified or compared at the global level.￼
