## Description

All about Expanded PVC - a great material for prototyping and building your engineering designs. I'm making this video because there's not yet any other introduction to prototyping with expanded pvc online.  This is popular in prototyping labs for multidisciplinary projects, but it's also a best-in-class choice for several engineering needs.  

What are the easiest ways to cut the pvc,  insert fasteners, drill holes?  How does it compare to acrylic and polycarbonate?  What adhesives are compatible?  How can you add color & texture? How does zero-kerf cutting reduce fabrication time?  How can you get a precise perpendicular edge?
