## Description

I've made about 20 significant changes to my closet / laundry / clothing habits so I will never again need to fold clothes.  I updated methods from the way I was raised, and re-engineered the system.   Today, I'm sharing this system with others who want to save time and skip the folding of clothes, gain space in the closet, and have an easier life. 

There are a few changes involved that require upgraded closet parts.  One of them is a fence post installed for the closet rod.  This is galvanized steel, much stronger than oak and won't bend under the load of all your wardrobe.  Then there's a 3D printed hanger that is used to load larger items and you can find it linked below.

I'm happy to hear your inputs on further improvements - all of your comments become new knowledge for the community who is watching along!
