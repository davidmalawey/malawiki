# Brey Caraway

SCUTTLE community contributor credited as the designer of the SCUTTLE payload deck — the 3D-printed twist-and-nub blocks, laser-cut acrylic top panel, and 30x30mm aluminum extrusion rails that capture a polyethylene cargo bin on top of the robot. Project published on Hackster.io.
