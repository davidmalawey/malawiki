# Kapton tape (polyimide)

NASA-origin polyimide tape, always yellow and semi-transparent. Tolerates soldering-temperature heat (~200 °C short periods), survives clean-room qualification, and is the industry standard for battery-pack and PCB thermal protection. Thickness ~45 µm; PET-based "heat transfer tape" is a thicker (~55 µm) alternative with slightly better adhesion.

**Price trick** ([[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]]): a data-sheet-certified roll runs ~$30; the same polymer unqualified on [[entities/brands/amazon|Amazon]] is $7-8. Usually not worth the premium unless you genuinely need the data sheet for a qualified project.

Limitations: one-shot adhesive (contaminates fast), does not stretch in two directions at once, nothing permanently bonds to it (molecular cousin of HDPE and nylon in that regard).
