# Steel d-rail

Off-the-shelf steel rail. David uses it in [[videos/aluminum-extrusions-fundamentals]] as the cheap alternative to running two matched horizontal [[entities/materials/aluminum|aluminum]] 3030 beams between vertical extrusion frames. Several horizontal d-rails cost less than one or two 3030 beams, they're easy to cut with a hand-lever cutting machine, and — critically — they don't require the same dimensional accuracy: if your cut is slightly long, you can squash the two vertical frames together or file the excess.
