# Urethane

Polyurethane. Mentioned in [[videos/borrow-a-tolerance-mindset-for-designers]] as an alternative source of cheap, tough polymer (commonly found in pneumatic hoses), comparable to [[entities/materials/hdpe|HDPE]] when you need to [[concepts/borrowing-tolerances|borrow toughness]] for a hinge or sliding joint. Both are very tough; HDPE edges ahead on low static friction.
