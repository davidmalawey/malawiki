# Cork

Compressible plant-fiber material. Used as three-point pads under a tabletop demo of humidity-driven bowing; also sold as 12-inch self-adhesive square sheets — naturally antimicrobial and antifungal, conforms to a shoe under body weight, outlasts the fabric layer of most retail insoles.
