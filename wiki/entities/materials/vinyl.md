# Vinyl / PVC

Flexible polymer. In [[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]], vinyl is the substrate for standard electrical tape and colored pinstripe tape — flexible, stretchable, easy to route around corners, but not suitable for high-temperature environments (will slide and become gummy). In [[videos/more-about-usb-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know]], vinyl shows up as the outer sheathing David cuts through to expose the conductors inside a USB cable.
