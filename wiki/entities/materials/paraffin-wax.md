# Paraffin wax

Grocery-store paraffin (sold in blocks for cheap) is David's preferred blade lubricant for cutting [[entities/materials/aluminum|aluminum]] T-slot extrusion on a [[entities/tools/miter-saw|miter saw]] with a regular wood blade. Rub the wax on the blade and include a chunk alongside the extrusion during the cut so the teeth keep getting re-coated. Same trick works on magnesium. David speculates a [[entities/brands/crayola|Crayola]] crayon or snowboard wax would be equivalent.

Without wax: localized melting, aluminum particles stuck to the teeth, smeared/ugly cut. With wax: near-factory cut quality.
