# HDPE (high-density polyethylene)

Very tough, very smooth polymer. Comes as cheap tubing for residential water systems — which is why [[videos/borrow-a-tolerance-mindset-for-designers]] treats it as the preferred source of "borrowed toughness and smoothness" for print-in-place hinges. Static friction coefficient is among the lowest of the plastics David compares (ABS, PLA, PETG, HDPE, nylon). Also comes up in [[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]] as the plastic-family exemplar of a non-porous, nothing-sticks-to-it surface (alongside the Kapton polymer).
