# PTFE / Teflon

Polytetrafluoroethylene. Homogeneous material with a grain direction — pull with the grain and it splits; pull against it and it narrows. Very low friction coefficient, very chemically resistant (gasoline, kerosene, acetone).

Primary use shown in [[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]] is pipe thread tape: seals tapered threads (e.g. pneumatic fittings, natural-gas pipe) by filling the wedge gap as the conical male/female threads interfere. **Does not help with straight threads**, and compression fittings seal via O-rings instead.

Thicker yellow variant is rated for combustible gases; generic white works for most plumbing. Also usable creatively — wedged between a rod and cylinder to reduce friction, or wrapped under a rivet head for a low-pressure fluid seal.
