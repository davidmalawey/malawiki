# Isopropyl alcohol

The first of David's "four horsemen of solvents" in [[videos/label-supplies-to-multiply-results]]. Removes [[entities/tools/sharpie|Sharpie]] from glossy / plastic surfaces effortlessly. Central to the labels workflow: because labels need to be changeable, you need a reliable solvent that doesn't damage the substrate.
