# Copper

The conductor inside USB cables. [[videos/more-about-usb-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know]] calls out the gauge difference that signals a quality cable: 22 AWG for power (red/black), 28 AWG for data (green/white). Better cables use gold-plated terminals; cheaper ones are nickel or tin plated. A cheap cable may have uniformly undersized 28 AWG on all conductors.
