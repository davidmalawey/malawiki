# Spring steel

The material David recommends "borrowing" when you need deterministic spring behavior — because plastic spring features are not deterministic. From [[videos/borrow-a-tolerance-mindset-for-designers]]: spring steel's elastic coefficient is stable across any temperature a human can survive, and a paper-clip-grade wire is manufactured to very tight diameter and flatness tolerances even though the manufacturer didn't care about your application.
