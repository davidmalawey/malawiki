# PVC

Polyvinyl chloride. Used as test fixture (rubber-band stretcher), as enclosure body (stock pipe sliced to length), as a substrate for solvent-welded PVC cement joints, and as a friction-weld substrate when bonded to ABS. David uses 10-inch PVC for FIFO sock chutes.
