# Arduino

Hobbyist microcontroller platform referenced in [[videos/more-about-usb-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know]] as the archetype for how students wire up projects — and as the site of David's most frequently observed student mistake: drawing actuator power (LED strips, motors) from the Arduino 5V pin and blaming the resulting voltage fluctuations on Wi-Fi. The 5V pin is sized for signal-level current, not amps.
