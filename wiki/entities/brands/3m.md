# 3M

Referenced as a quality benchmark in both the tape and labels videos. 3M's double-sided foam tapes and mounting adhesives are called out by the trademark embossed pattern; their data sheets are cited as the reason to pay a premium for name-brand over generic. In the labels video, 3M respirators / safety masks come up as the brand a manual might list — but an equivalent $10 piece of PPE can often be substituted if you read the standards (ASM-compliant).
