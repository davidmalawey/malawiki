# Ridgid

Cordless power-tool maker. Cited as more honest in voltage/capacity ratings than peers; David uses Ridgid specifically because of the lifetime battery warranty. Their portable battery shop vacuum is also one reference connector for the parametric vacuum adapter, and David benchmarks his open-source battery adapter against their $89 USB-C PD unit.
