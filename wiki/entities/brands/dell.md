# Dell

PC integrator. Central case study in David's "OEM of Modules" essay — Dell designs almost nothing itself; the components (Intel/AMD CPU, motherboard, RAM, drives, OS) are sourced from upstream specialists, and the value-add is integration, support, and brand. Recurs in the open-source-hardware-is-evolving thesis as the PC-industry analog of where modular open-source robotics could go.
