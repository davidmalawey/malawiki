# MPU-9250

9-axis IMU (3-axis accelerometer, 3-axis gyro, 3-axis magnetometer) on the [[entities/tools/beaglebone-blue|BeagleBone Blue]]. SCUTTLE's compass-heading routine reads the magnetometer over its built-in I2C path.
