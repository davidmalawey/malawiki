# P1AM-100

[[entities/brands/automation-direct|AutomationDirect]] P1AM-100 — an Arduino-compatible PLC controller that uses Productivity1000 expansion modules. Programmed via [[entities/tools/productivity-blocks|Productivity Blocks]] (graphical) or directly in the [[entities/tools/arduino-ide|Arduino IDE]]. Central controller in the Texas A&M MXET 400 conveyor demo.
