# Hobby servo

Generic 180-degree RC-style hobby servo (M2.5/M3 mounting screws, varying body dimensions across vendors). SCUTTLE uses them for cheap, off-the-shelf joints — the BeagleBone Blue can drive up to 8 directly. Used in the SCUTTLE servo-arm v1 design.
