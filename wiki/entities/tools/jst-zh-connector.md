# JST-ZH connector

1.5 mm pitch 2-pin signal connector. Used on the [[entities/tools/beaglebone-blue|BeagleBone Blue]]'s motor PWM signal outputs; SCUTTLE harnesses adapt JST-ZH on the BBB side to [[entities/tools/dupont-connector|Dupont]] on the motor-driver side.
