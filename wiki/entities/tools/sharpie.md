# Sharpie

The default marking tool across David's videos. Recurring specifics:

- On white medical / masking tape — use a *finer* Sharpie because fiber-backed tapes cause bleed ([[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]]).
- On nylon and HDPE containers — the "fat" marker (a thicker-bodied variant) is the one that actually adheres well, and both can be erased with [[entities/materials/isopropyl-alcohol|isopropyl alcohol]], the first of the "four horsemen of solvents" ([[videos/label-supplies-to-multiply-results]]).
- On silver / anodized-gray aluminum extrusion — Sharpie works well and comes off quickly with acetone, while pencil is difficult to fully remove ([[videos/aluminum-extrusions-fundamentals]]).
