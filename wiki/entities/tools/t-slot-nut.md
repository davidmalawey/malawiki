# T-slot nut

M6 (or other size) slide-in nut that drops into the channel of an aluminum T-slot extrusion to anchor brackets and mounts. Foundational fastener for the [[entities/tools/2020-extrusion|2020]] / [[entities/tools/3030-extrusion|3030]] extrusion ecosystem.
