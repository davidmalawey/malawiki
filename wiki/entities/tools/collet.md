# Collet

Precision-clamping alternative to a 3-jaw chuck for high-RPM tools. Introduced in [[videos/borrow-a-tolerance-mindset-for-designers]] as the archetype of distributing clamp deviations across many contact points so the clamped item's center line moves less than any single jaw's deviation — the same principle as [[entities/tools/ball-bearings|ball bearings]] applied to a tool holder.
