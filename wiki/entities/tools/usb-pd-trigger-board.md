# USB-PD trigger board

Small breakout that negotiates a fixed voltage (5/9/12/15/20 V) from a USB-PD wall adapter over USB-C. David uses one to deliver 12 V at 30 W to his bench from any PD adapter, then taps a $1 regulator board on its output for the 5 V rail.
