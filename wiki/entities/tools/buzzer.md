# Buzzer

Piezoelectric buzzer used as an MQTT-driven IoT demo actuator — a phone, PC, or another robot publishes a topic and the SCUTTLE-mounted buzzer chirps. Cheap, easy-to-hear feedback for [[concepts/m2m-iot-communication|M2M-IoT]] tutorials.
