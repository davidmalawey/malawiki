# Hobby ESC

Off-the-shelf RC-car-grade Electronic Speed Control for brushed DC motors. Driven by 50 Hz / 1-2 ms hobby-servo PWM. The unit David shows is rated 320 A and cost roughly $5-10. Used on early SCUTTLE as an alternative to a discrete H-bridge for driving 12 V gearmotors from a Raspberry Pi.
