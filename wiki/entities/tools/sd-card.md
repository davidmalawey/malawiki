# SD card

Generic microSD storage. SCUTTLE's [[entities/tools/beaglebone-blue|BeagleBone Blue]] boots from a 16 GB SD card with the partition expanded from ~3.6 GB to 14.5 GB by the SCUTTLE self-installer.
