# BeagleBone Blue

Single-board Linux computer (Octavo OSD3358 SoC, 512 MB DDR3, 4 GB eMMC) with on-board 2x H-bridges, encoders, IMU, and LiPo charger. The native [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] platform throughout 2019-2020, before the project added Raspberry Pi as an alternative host. Distinct from the later [[entities/tools/beaglebone-y-ai|BeagleBone Y-AI]] AI variant.
