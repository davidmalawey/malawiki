# Circular saw

Recurs in two specific workflows:

- Tape down the cut line on composite material before running the saw — the blade's tooth, as it comes up through the material, lifts fibers and causes splintering; masking tape prevents that ([[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]]).
- Run a plain wood blade through [[entities/materials/aluminum|aluminum]] or magnesium using [[entities/materials/paraffin-wax|paraffin wax]] as the lubricant — prevents the blade teeth from smearing aluminum around and gives near-factory cut quality ([[videos/aluminum-extrusions-fundamentals]]).
