# Relay module

Multi-channel 24 V (or 12 V) relay module mounted on a DIN-rail bracket. In the MXET demo two relays are wired in series with a Dorner conveyor's existing manual on/off and direction switches so the PLC can override them. Also reused on SprayMaster as a $1 12 V switching element for a linear actuator.
