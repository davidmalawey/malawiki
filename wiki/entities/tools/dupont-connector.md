# Dupont connector

0.1" pitch crimp-and-housing connector system used on Raspberry Pi / Arduino headers. David recommends ribbon-style multi-pin housings over individual jumpers. A Dupont housings kit (assortment of housings, crimp pins, and header pins) lets him convert 'incompatible terminals' between breakout boards into pluggable, reworkable connections.
