# Drill bit

Generic twist drill bit used to chase printed axle holes and other post-print hole sizing. SCUTTLE uses an 8.0 mm metric bit to bring printed wheel-axle holes to the press-fit dimension, taking advantage of [[concepts/plastic-compressibility|plastic compressibility]].
