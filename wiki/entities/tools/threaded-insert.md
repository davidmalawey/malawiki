# Threaded insert

Brass insert pressed (with heat) into plastic to provide a reusable threaded hole. Standard upgrade for any 3D-printed fastener point. David recommends them over self-tapping screws for any fastener removed more than a few times. Standard depth ~6 mm drives David's 6 mm panel-thickness preference.
