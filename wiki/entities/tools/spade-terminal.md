# Spade terminal

Female quick-disconnect terminal. Color-coded by wire size: pink (16 AWG), blue (14 AWG). Crimped on stranded wire and slotted onto a male tab. Often paired with [[entities/materials/heat-shrink|heat-shrink]] for strain relief and insulation.
