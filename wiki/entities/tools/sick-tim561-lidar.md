# SICK TiM561 lidar

Industrial 2D scanning lidar from SICK with an aluminum housing and M3 mount points. Used on early SCUTTLE chassis for nearest-obstacle scanning before being replaced by the cheaper [[entities/tools/rplidar-a1|RPLIDAR A1]].
