# SCUTTLE Asia (Malaysia lab space)

SCUTTLE Robotics deployment in Malaysia — Johor Bahru office suite with two 3D printers, robots, and an aquarium. Running an undergrad mechatronics curriculum with state-government and corporate sponsorship; producing novel working robotic prototypes in <6 months as of early 2025.
