# Assist-as-needed rehabilitation exoskeleton

Robotic exoskeleton for rehabilitation, developed in [[entities/people/reza-langari|Dr. Reza Langari]]'s lab at Texas A&M. Each joint contains a torque sensor and a [[entities/tools/harmonic-drive|harmonic-drive]] gearbox; control loop fuses joint torque with [[entities/tools/emg-sensor|EMG]] bio-signals to assist only the residual force the patient can't produce.
