# CubeSat MDO thesis (2016)

David's 2016 master's research artifact — a multidisciplinary design optimization study of CubeSat structures using a [[concepts/genetic-algorithm-plus-gradient|two-stage GA + gradient]] optimizer, [[concepts/finite-element-analysis|FEA]] convergence study, and [[concepts/sensitivity-analysis|sensitivity analysis]] across competing objectives (cost vs. mass). Foundational to David's later "design the best in the world" / "optimal ecosystem" thinking.
