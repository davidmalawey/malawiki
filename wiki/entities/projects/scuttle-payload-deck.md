# SCUTTLE payload deck

Open-source SCUTTLE payload accessory designed by [[entities/people/brey-caraway|Brey Caraway]] — a deck made of 3D-printed twist-and-nub blocks, a laser-cut [[entities/materials/acrylic|acrylic]] top panel, and 30x30 mm aluminum extrusion rails that captures a polyethylene cargo bin on top of the robot. Demonstrated on a payload-robot SCUTTLE v2.4 build.
