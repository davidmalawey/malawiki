# SCUTTLE IoT ecosystem

The networked-SCUTTLE concept umbrella — phones, PCs, ESP8266 nodes, and SCUTTLE robots all publishing/subscribing to shared [[concepts/mqtt|MQTT]] topics. David's preliminary IoT guide covers topic-naming conventions and demonstrates four heterogeneous clients exchanging messages through one broker.
