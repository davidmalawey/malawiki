# SCUTTLE hinge family

Multi-variant parametric printable hinge family for SCUTTLE Robotics. Three published variants — v3 (beefy single-pin), v2 (flush-panel pair, 10 kg-rated), and hinge-double (light-load articulation). Hosted on GrabCAD as fan_joint-1.
