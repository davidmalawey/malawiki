# OpenBox project

David's open-source ecosystem of 3D-printable inserts and accessories built around standard North American electrical [[entities/tools/handy-box|handy boxes]] as a universal mounting/enclosure platform. Documented at qr.net/openboxproject and on GitHub. Hosts parametric printable adapters, [[entities/tools/anderson-connector|Anderson connectors]], and DC power sources.
