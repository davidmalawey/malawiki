# SprayMaster

Sub-$25 open-design SCUTTLE add-on featuring a [[entities/tools/laser-pointer|laser pointer]] aim, a $1 [[entities/tools/relay-module|relay module]], a 12 V [[entities/tools/linear-actuator|linear actuator]], and a Raspberry Pi for control. Mounted on [[entities/tools/2020-extrusion|2020 extrusion]] using the same standardized interface as the rest of the SCUTTLE accessory family. Published at `hackster.io/dmalawey/spraymaster-6212d0`.
