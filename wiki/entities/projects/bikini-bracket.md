# Bikini bracket

Open-source 3D-printable ABS bracket whose geometry occupies the negative space between three tangent circles. Designed to bond to PVC tube with PVC cement for low-cost modular lab/storage assemblies. Hosted on GrabCAD as bikini_bracket-1.
