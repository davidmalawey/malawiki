# Team Zamalik chili-detection robot

Malaysian SCUTTLE-platform undergraduate field robot (Dec 2024) — 4WD agricultural prototype with two cameras (detection + navigation), Wi-Fi, ML chili-disease detection, audio alerts, and remote-controlled fertilizer spray system. Built in under 6 months.
