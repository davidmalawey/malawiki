# OpenLab project

David's umbrella open-source publication of his multidisciplinary engineering lab — design files, actuator inventories, parametric designs, methodology PDFs, curriculum, tools, supplies, and methods. Hosted at qr.net/openlabproject and github.com/davidmalawey/openLab. Reaches ~90% of the outcomes of all the specialized labs combined.
