# Design by questions

## Definition

Reframing mechanical design as a finite list of discrete questions, each answered by a benchmark, standard, or mating-part measurement, so that non-specialists can produce sturdy parts. Variant filenames (sleeve, sleeve-BR, sleeve-BR-inch) encode which questions a given variant answers.
