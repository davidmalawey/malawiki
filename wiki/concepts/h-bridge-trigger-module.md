# H-bridge trigger module

## Definition

Four-switch (typically MOSFET) circuit that lets a DC supply drive a brushed DC motor in either direction at variable speed. In a cordless drill the module sits between battery and motor with a trigger lever modulating duty cycle, while a benchtop equivalent is a discrete dual-channel motor-driver board.
