# Vibration damping

## Definition

Reducing resonant oscillation by changing the mass, stiffness, or internal friction of a structure. Thin-wall enclosures ring like drums; adding mass or lossy material shifts the resonant frequency lower and kills ring-down faster.

## How David uses it

In [[videos/more-than-you-ever-wanted-to-know-about-tape]], David demonstrates a cheap-appliance panel that reverberates when struck, then kills the ring by adhering a mass of weighted steel backed with double-sided foam tape to the inside. He also draws the analogy to drummer weights placed inside acoustic drums.

Quantitative intuition from the same video (for foam tape as a spring): doubling thickness halves the spring constant (same force deflects 2× further); doubling bonded surface area halves the deflection under the same force.
