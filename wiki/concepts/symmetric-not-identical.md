# Symmetric, not identical

## Definition

A SCUTTLE recurring design language where left-and-right parts are functionally mirrored (forcing the assembler to pay attention to orientation) versus parts that are identical (same print, fits either side). David trades off the cost of two distinct prints against the cost of mis-orientation; rod brackets are identical to reduce SKU count, encoder/wheel brackets are symmetric.
