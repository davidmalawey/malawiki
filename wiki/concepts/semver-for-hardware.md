# Semver for hardware

## Definition

Major.minor revision scheme David applies to SCUTTLE assemblies and parts (e.g. v2.4, v3.0). Major bumps signal interface changes; minor bumps signal compatible internal improvements. Adapts software semver to physical assemblies — printed parts ship with the version debossed in.
