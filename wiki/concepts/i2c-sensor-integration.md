# I2C sensor integration

## Definition

Reading a digital sensor (here, a [[entities/tools/vl53-distance-sensor|VL53 ToF]]) over I2C from a microcontroller ([[entities/tools/esp8266-nodemcu|NodeMCU/ESP8266]]) and republishing the threshold result as a digital GPIO line for downstream consumers.
