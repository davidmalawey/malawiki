# Benchmarking-driven design

## Definition

First step before CAD: find an existing high-volume product whose layout already solves most of the problem and copy its decisions. Saves the cost of mistakes other designers already paid. David applies it specifically to the conduit/electrical aisle and to the bestselling product in any category.
