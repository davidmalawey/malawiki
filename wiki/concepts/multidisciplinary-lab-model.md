# Multidisciplinary lab model

## Definition

Operating pattern for a sustainable undergrad hands-on engineering lab: continuity across semesters, ~5–6 active projects, student-led management with faculty oversight, mix of capstone/sponsored/student-initiated work, weekly report-outs, modular workspaces, and at least one faculty member with both hands-on building experience and external-business interfacing experience.
