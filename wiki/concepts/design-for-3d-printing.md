# Design for 3D printing

## Definition

Methodology distinct from injection-molding-oriented design — virtually no incumbent does it. 3D-print-first design tolerates non-uniform wall thickness, designs around layer adhesion direction, exploits zero-cost custom geometry, and accepts looser tolerance in exchange for parametric speed.
