# Threshold-switching control

## Definition

Switch between two control modes ("drive straight" vs "follow a curve") based on whether a tracked angle gap exceeds a span threshold. An alternative to PID for problems with two clearly-defined regimes — appears in [[videos/navigationvectors-part2-curve-criteria|NavigationVectors part 2]].
