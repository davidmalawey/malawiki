# Phillips size as torque limiter

## Definition

Design-intent reading of the Phillips screwdriver's notorious cam-out behavior: the size mismatch between PH0/PH1/PH2/PH3 and the screw is a torque limiter, not a defect. Pick the smaller bit when you want to feel the torque ceiling; pick the larger when you want to push past it.
