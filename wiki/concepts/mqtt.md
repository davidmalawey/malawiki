# MQTT

## Definition

Lightweight publish/subscribe messaging protocol for IoT — clients connect to a broker, publish to topics, and subscribe to topics. SCUTTLE's IoT arc uses [[entities/tools/hivemq|HiveMQ]]'s public broker as the cloud relay between phones, PCs, ESP8266 nodes, and SCUTTLE robots.
