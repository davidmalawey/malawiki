# Design commonization

## Definition

Reusing the same printed part (green bracket, blue cap, purple snap) at multiple joints of an assembly to reduce part-count and BOM complexity. Sibling of [[concepts/lab-commonization|lab-commonization]] applied at the assembly scale.
