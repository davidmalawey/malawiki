# DC motor fundamentals

## Definition

Working principles of brushed DC motors as understood through teardown — voltage drives current through nominal armature resistance to produce torque; brushless variants share the same system role but spin faster and last longer; the same architecture scales from a hand drill (18 V) to a treadmill motor.
