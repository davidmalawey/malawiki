# Hotkey-bound OCR

## Definition

Productivity pattern for ad-hoc text capture: bind a hotkey (via [[entities/tools/sharex|ShareX]] or similar) to "screenshot region → OCR → clipboard." Removes the friction of retyping any text on screen, including text in images, screenshots, and videos.
