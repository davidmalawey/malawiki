# Single-pump aquaponics

## Definition

Engineered water levels across subsystems eliminate redundant pumps — one pump moves water from the lowest reservoir through every stage of an aquaponics loop, with elevation differences doing the rest. [[entities/projects/opengrow|OpenGrow]] is the worked example.
