# USB-PD vs. 5 V power

## Definition

The distinction between USB-PD adapters that negotiate higher voltages (12 V, 15 V, 20 V) and legacy USB adapters stuck at 5 V; modern SBCs accept USB-C connectors but stay on 5 V, requiring 3+ A through a connector most adapters only deliver 2 A on.
