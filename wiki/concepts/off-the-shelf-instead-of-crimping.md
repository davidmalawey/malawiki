# Off-the-shelf instead of crimping

## Definition

Before crimping any small-pitch terminal, check the catalog for pre-crimped jumpers, [[entities/brands/futaba|Futaba]]-style servo leads, or 22 AWG single-cord wire that plug into the same socket. The crimp is often unnecessary engineering overhead.
