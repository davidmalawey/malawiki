# C rate

Discharge-rate metric defined as the current that empties a full battery in one hour, used to compare chemistries independent of capacity. Recurs across SCUTTLE power content alongside [[concepts/discharge-profile]] and [[concepts/battery-chemistry-tradeoffs]].
