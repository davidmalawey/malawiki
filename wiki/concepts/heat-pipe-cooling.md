# Heat-pipe cooling

## Definition

Cooling technique using a sealed copper tube with a working fluid that vaporizes at the hot end, travels to the cold end, condenses, and returns via capillary action along the tube wall. Enables passive high-performance heat transport in modern compact PC coolers.
