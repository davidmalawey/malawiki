# Working placeholder design

## Definition

Ship a documented mediocre design rather than a hidden great one. Imperfect open work invites contribution; perfect hidden work invites nothing. David's framing for why he publishes the [[entities/projects/openspin|OpenSpin]] 3D-printed bearing despite knowing it can be improved.
