# Prototype / product / commodity spectrum

## Definition

David's three-regime model of mechanical parts. **Prototype** = high price + fine tolerance + few engineering hours. **Commodity** = low price + fine tolerance + decades of competitive refinement. **3D print** = low price + looser tolerance + custom geometry. Use commodities for tolerance, 3D prints for connection geometry, prototypes only when necessary.
