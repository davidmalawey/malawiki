# Power-budget method

## Definition

Spreadsheet-driven workflow for projecting peak power, average power, runtime, and power-source margin across an entire mechatronics build. Tabs separate goals, electronics data, runtime, system power map, exclusions, and justifications. Lets a team trim deliverables to the calculations that actually matter.
