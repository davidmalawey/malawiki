# PC evolution analogy for robotics

## Definition

Recurring framing device. Robotics today resembles the PC market of the early 1990s — most makers integrate vertically, components are tightly coupled, prices are high, and failure modes are opaque. The PC market shifted to module-OEM economics ([[entities/brands/dell|Dell]] designs nothing); robotics is on the same trajectory.
