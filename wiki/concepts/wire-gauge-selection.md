# Wire-gauge selection

## Definition

Choosing wire size based on continuous current and connector compatibility. 18 AWG is rated to roughly 16 A; above that David rethinks the design rather than going thicker. Wire gauge constrains terminal selection (pink spade for 16 AWG, blue for 14 AWG; ferrule sleeves color-coded by gauge).
