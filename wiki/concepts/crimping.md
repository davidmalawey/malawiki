# Crimping

## Definition

Compressing a metal terminal onto stripped copper wire so the joint is gas-tight, low-resistance, and mechanically retained without solder. Each terminal family has its own crimper, die, and orientation rules; the goal is the same — make a joint that survives a tug test.
