# Counterweight design

## Definition

Adding mass behind a servo's pivot to reduce torque demand at the load end. SCUTTLE's [[entities/projects/scuttle-servo-arm-v1|servo arm v1]] uses stackable hockey pucks (172 g each, 1-4 pucks) — a cheap source of disc-shaped tunable mass.
