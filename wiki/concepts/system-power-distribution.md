# System power distribution

## Definition

Architecture-level layout of how power flows from wall AC through e-stop, switch, AC/DC supplies, and DIN-rail terminal blocks to PLC bus, output modules, and relays. Includes which terminal blocks are bridged for ground vs. hot.
