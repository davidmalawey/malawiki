# Free upgrades

## Definition

A cost-neutral improvement: same price, better outcome. David's recurring "you're going to buy hot glue anyway — buy the black sticks" framing — leveraging fungibility-of-options to upgrade by default. Sibling of [[concepts/kaizen|kaizen]] applied to consumables.
