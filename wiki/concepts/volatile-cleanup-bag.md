# Volatile-cleanup bag

## Definition

Before starting a job involving acetone, epoxy, or other volatiles, line a tin can with a doggy-duty bag; ball up wipes and gloves into it within ~60 seconds of finishing and tie off, keeping fumes from spreading through the lab.
