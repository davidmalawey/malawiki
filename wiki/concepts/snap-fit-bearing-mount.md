# Snap-fit bearing mount

## Definition

Three small compression nubs printed into a pocket grip the inner race of a 608 ball bearing; the outer race remains free to spin. Allows fastener-free bearing capture — extension of [[concepts/three-point-clamping|three-point clamping]] applied to FDM.
