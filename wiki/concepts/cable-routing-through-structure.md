# Cable routing through structure

## Definition

Running servo (or other) wires through internal channels of brackets and down the bore of structural tubing, so the cable never crosses a joint sweep and never snags. Sibling of [[concepts/aluminum-extrusion-wiring|aluminum-extrusion wiring]] applied to round tube.
