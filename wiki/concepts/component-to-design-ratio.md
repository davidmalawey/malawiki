# Component-to-design ratio

## Definition

Central concept in David's "OEM of Modules" essay. The ratio of how much of a finished product is bought-in modules vs. designed in-house. Modern PCs have a near-zero design ratio; modern robots have a near-1.0 ratio. Lowering the ratio unlocks open-source hardware economics.
