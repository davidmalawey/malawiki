# Bio-inspired dynamics

## Definition

David's six-principle framework for what modern robots are missing relative to biological systems: small-mass-first, no-base-reaction, center-of-gravity conservation, force pre-loading, angular-momentum conservation, and vision-not-encoders. Foreshadows OpenArm and the parametric-design + AI thesis.
