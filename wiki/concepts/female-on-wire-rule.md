# Female-on-wire rule

## Definition

Connector safety rule: put female terminals on the wire and male pins on the board. The board can be fastened; the wires sling. A male pin flailing on a loose wire will short something it touches. Holds for [[entities/tools/dupont-connector|Dupont]] and similar header-style connectors.
