# PWM (pulse width modulation)

## Definition

Switching technique that delivers controllable average voltage to a DC motor by toggling supply at a chosen duty cycle and frequency. David covers how frequency choice affects audible noise, motor heating, and driver efficiency, and how servo PWM (50 Hz, 1–2 ms pulses) differs from motor-driver PWM.
