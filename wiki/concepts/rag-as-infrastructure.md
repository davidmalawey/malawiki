# Rag as infrastructure

## Definition

Treating cotton/microfiber rags as a managed lab consumable backed by a commercial laundry-rotation contract (Cintas/Grainger-style). Foundational for solvent work, finishing, and basic cleanliness; absence cascades into substandard student outcomes.
