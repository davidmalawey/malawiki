# Die orientation rule

## Definition

For [[entities/tools/anderson-connector|Anderson Powerwerx]] crimps, hold the terminal with the tongue pointing down and rest it on the floor of the matching 15A/30A/45A die before squeezing. Wrong orientation deforms the contact and prevents the housing from latching.
