# Differential-drive kinematics

## Definition

Two-wheel drive geometry: linear velocity X-dot and angular velocity theta-dot derived from individual wheel speeds (right vs left) and wheelbase. Foundation for SCUTTLE's [[videos/navigationvectors-part1-global-position-increment|NavigationVectors]] code.
