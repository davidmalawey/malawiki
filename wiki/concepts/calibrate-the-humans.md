# Calibrate the humans

## Definition

David's name for the technique of writing a measured value on a visible label and *not asking anyone to study it*. Passers-by absorb the numeric intuition subconsciously. A label that says "Setting 3 = 5 m/s" next to a fan quietly trains everyone in the lab what 5 m/s feels like, so when they later spec a fan for an enclosure they have the reference already.

## How David uses it

Introduced in [[videos/label-supplies-to-multiply-results]] as one of the highest-leverage uses of label-making. An extension of the same idea: leaving the measured spec on a product's surface so the next student doesn't have to hunt the datasheet.
