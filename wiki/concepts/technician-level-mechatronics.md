# Technician-level mechatronics

## Definition

Proposed degree philosophy David sketches: a technician-track mechatronics program focused on assembling and maintaining open modules rather than designing from chip up. Bridges the EE/ME divide using shared interfaces ([[concepts/standards-as-open-source|standards as open source]]) instead of requiring deep specialization in either.
