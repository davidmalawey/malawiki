## Overview

Fifty-second shop hack: David fixes the problem of light, slippery soldering-iron tip cleaners by gluing the cheap cleaner basket into a Harbor Freight magnetic parts dish using silicone adhesive. The dish adds mass so the cleaner stops sliding around, and the magnetic base holds it on a steel work surface.

## Key takeaways

- Modern soldering-iron cleaners have gotten cheaper and lighter — they slide around in use.
- A $3 Harbor Freight magnetic parts dish makes a perfect heavy base.
- Silicone adhesive bonds the cleaner to the dish but stays reversible — if it peels off it peels off clean.
- The magnetic base resists sliding even on a dusty bench.
