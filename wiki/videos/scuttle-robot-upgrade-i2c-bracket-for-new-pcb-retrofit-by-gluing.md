## Overview

Quick retrofit clip: bond the new, more rigid i2C bracket to the existing BeagleBone bracket using PVC glue applied with a syringe for control.

## Key takeaways

- PVC glue bonds [[entities/materials/abs|ABS]] plastic about as fast as super glue when there is no large gap to fill.
- A syringe gives controlled application of a small amount of adhesive on a flat mating surface.
- The new i2C board replaces an older clip-on design and is more compact and rigid.

## Materials used

- [[entities/materials/pvc-cement|PVC cement]] (primary adhesive)
- [[entities/materials/abs|ABS]] (bracket substrate)
- [[entities/materials/super-glue|Super glue]] (mentioned as comparable alternative)
