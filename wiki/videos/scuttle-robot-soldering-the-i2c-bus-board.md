## Overview

A short [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] build clip showing a practical hack for soldering a multi-pin header to the I2C bus PCB without helping hands, then walking through normal soldering practice with attention to flux melt and continuity checking.

## Key takeaways

- Press the plastic header against the [[entities/tools/soldering-iron|soldering iron]] tip briefly (about 10 seconds) to fuse the plastic spacer to the PCB, holding the pins vertical without helping hands.
- Apply the iron to the joint on the side opposite already-soldered pins so the heat path doesn't reflow earlier work.
- Heat both pad and pin before feeding solder so it wicks properly.
- Liquid spreading between pins after soldering is usually melted flux, not a short — verify with a [[entities/tools/multimeter|multimeter]] continuity check.
