## Overview

A short clip demonstrating that a solvent-based adhesive (Pro Weld) can wick into and fill small gaps between two pieces of [[entities/materials/acrylic|acrylic]]. David shows slow-motion footage of the solvent flowing between mating surfaces and notes that two brushstrokes were needed because the first pass left insufficient solvent volume.

## Key takeaways

- Solvent-based adhesives can fill small gaps between plastic parts by capillary action and chemical softening.
- Watch the solvent flow visually — if it doesn't fully wet the joint, apply a second pass.
- The Pro Weld product is sold for styrene, butadiene, and acrylic, but off-the-shelf [[entities/materials/acetone|acetone]] (or another suitable solvent) would likely work just as well.
