## Overview

[[entities/people/david-malawey|David]] demonstrates his [[entities/projects/openlab-project|OpenLab]] spill-prep kit and methodology after a power-steering-fluid spill. Layered approach: [[entities/materials/chemsorb|Chemsorb]] absorbent powder (decanted into a protein-shaker bottle for one-handed dispensing) for chemicals, paper-shredder paper or [[entities/materials/sawdust|sawdust]] for bulk spills, a [[entities/tools/honeywell-hpa300|Honeywell HPA300 HEPA filter]] on turbo for vapors, and a magnet-mounted [[entities/tools/toolbox-trash-bin|toolbox trash bin]] system around the workshop so the trash bag is always within arm's reach. Bonus tip: cut a roll of shop towels in half so the small one is what's at hand.

## Key takeaways

- Convert liquid spills into solid powder waste before cleaning — sprinkle absorbent, smear with cardboard, then sweep up.
- [[entities/materials/chemsorb|Chemsorb]] is documented and traceable for engineering write-ups; [[entities/materials/sawdust|sawdust]] from your [[entities/tools/miter-saw|miter saw]] dust bag (dedicate a clean run to one wood species) does most of the same job for free.
- Paper-shredder shreds work nearly as well as sawdust for non-aggressive spills (e.g. spilled milk → compost).
- For volatile cleanup (acetone, epoxy), pre-stage a tin can lined with a doggy-duty bag; ball up wipes and gloves into it within 60 seconds and tie off — keeps fumes out of the room.
- Then run the [[entities/tools/honeywell-hpa300|Honeywell HPA300]] HEPA filter on turbo to clear residual vapors in ~2 minutes; replaceable filter elements make these cheap to maintain.
- Standardized magnetic [[entities/tools/toolbox-trash-bin|trash-bin toolboxes]] with a threaded-rivet drop-on-screw mount let bags be swapped without trips outside; a rubber lip dampens contact noise.
- Cover the paper-towel dispenser with a paint-can lid plus 1-inch hole — keeps dust and rain off rolls left outside.
- A protein shaker bottle is a cheap, reusable shaker for any granular shop material.
