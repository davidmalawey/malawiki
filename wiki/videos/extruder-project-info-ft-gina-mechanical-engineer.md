## Overview

[[entities/people/david-malawey|David]] introduces [[entities/people/gina|Gina]], a fourth-year mechanical engineering student in Canada, who self-organized a GoFundMe to build a filament extruder for SCUTTLE Robotics students at FUTO in Nigeria. Gina narrates how the extruder works (pellets and shredded plastic → heated barrel → screw → filament strand), why Nigerian shipping makes commercial filament unreliable, and why local production unlocks self-sufficiency for the robotics lab.

## Key takeaways

- A filament extruder converts plastic pellets and shredded waste into 3D-printer feedstock via a screw pushing material through a heated barrel.
- Nigeria's shipping unreliability and lack of filament supply makes a local extruder a force-multiplier for the SCUTTLE Robotics Nigeria lab.
- Gina exemplifies the leadership trait David prizes: she discovered the project online, identified what was needed, and acted without waiting for step-by-step instruction.
- Fundraiser surplus goes to additional robotics-lab equipment.
