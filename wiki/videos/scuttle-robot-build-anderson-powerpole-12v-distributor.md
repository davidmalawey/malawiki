## Overview

Recipe-style clip showing how to build a 12V power distributor for [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] using [[entities/tools/anderson-connector|Anderson Powerpole]] connectors and 18 AWG silicone-insulated wire.

## Key takeaways

- Cut wires to 256 mm and strip 8 mm from each end; final wire goes 130 mm.
- First crimp uses a 15 A terminal; remaining crimps use 30 A terminals.
- Insert an empty housing between connections to space them apart for clean alignment.
