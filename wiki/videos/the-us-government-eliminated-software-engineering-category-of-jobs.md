## Overview

A short news-flash style essay. David notices the US Bureau of Labor Statistics' [Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/occupation-finder.htm) — which in 2007/2009 listed "computer software engineer" as one of the fastest-growing occupations — no longer carries that category in 2022. Computer Engineer (hardware) still ranks at the top by median pay, but software engineering has effectively dissolved into every other discipline. Two reasons: (1) every other engineering bachelor's now includes software as a tool, and (2) the most productive software engineers now write software that writes software.

## Key takeaways

- The BLS reclassified or eliminated the standalone "computer software engineer" category between ~2009 and 2022.
- Software became a tool — like a calculator, like Excel — that every engineer now uses.
- Mechanical engineers still build sensors and devices, and those devices contain software, so MEs need software fluency too.
- The best software engineers today are writing meta-software (e.g. smartphone automations let users *configure* software without writing code, which is itself a form of programming).
- Implication: the category exploded so successfully that "everyone is a software engineer" — making the standalone job title less meaningful. Mechanical engineering is "coming back" as a label because it's the discipline that hasn't dissolved.
