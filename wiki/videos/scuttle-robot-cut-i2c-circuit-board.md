## Overview

A 35-second clip showing how to cut a perforated prototyping PCB at the J-row to size it down to ten rows for the [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] I2C bus board.

## Key takeaways

- The board's silkscreen letter rows act as ready-made guides — placing the blade directly on the J row yields a clean ten-row strip.
- Use grid coordinates on prototyping PCBs to plan cuts deterministically.
