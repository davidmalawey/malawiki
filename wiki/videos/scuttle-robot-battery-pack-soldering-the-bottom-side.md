## Overview

How to solder the bottom-side wires of the SCUTTLE [[entities/tools/18650-cell|18650]] 3-cell battery holder. David shows the U-shape strand prep, rosin flux pen application, and the iron technique to wet steel pins that otherwise sink heat away too quickly.

## Key takeaways

- Cut the 85 mm wire, strip 10 mm from each end.
- **Twist the strands gently and fold them into a U-shape.** This stacks the odds in your favor because the fine 18 AWG wire doesn't solder reliably to the steel battery-holder pins - the heat dissipates outward in too many directions.
- Apply [[entities/materials/rosin-flux|rosin flux]] from a flux pen to both the U and the pin before soldering.
- **Heat the joint first**, then feed the solder. If the solder doesn't flow within a couple of seconds, give it more heat and try again.
- Flow plenty of solder so no loose strands remain visible from the top side - that's the most important quality criterion.
- Trade-off: heating long enough to fully wet the joint will partly melt the conductor near the joint. Cosmetic only - doesn't degrade the connection.
- **Don't take too much wire on the first bend** or you won't have enough length on the second bend - the 85 mm spec is a strict minimum.
