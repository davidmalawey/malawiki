## Overview

Mechanical walkthrough of pressing [[entities/tools/ball-bearings|ball bearings]] into a fresh pair of [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] wheels by hand and stacking the axle hardware (washer, spring washer, bolt). David covers the role of the small steel inner-race spacer tube — it prevents the bolt from over-compressing the bearing inner races — and notes what to do when the kit ships without one.

## Key takeaways

- Bearings press in by hand into either side of the wheel — orientation doesn't matter.
- The kit's small steel tube is a spacer that protects the inner races from being crushed when the axle bolt is tightened.
- If the spacer is missing, just don't apply much torque to the axle bolt.
- The flat washer should only contact the bearing inner race, never the outer race or the wheel body, to avoid drag.
- Stack on the inside is: pulley (glued) -> wheel -> bearings -> flat washer -> spring washer -> bolt -> nut.
