## Overview

How to make a reliable crimp on a female insulated spade terminal (18-22 AWG) for the SCUTTLE battery pack. David contrasts a proper crimper - one with two distinct hump features (location A and location B) - against the common cheap Harbor-Freight ratcheting style, which lacks the hump and won't form a good u-shape crimp on these terminals.

## Key takeaways

- Strip ~6 mm of insulation. The crimper's stripping jaw automatically gauges this when fully closed.
- After stripping, **gently twist** the strands to keep the conductor together; you need every last strand for the wire to carry its rated current.
- Use the correct gauge hole - 18 AWG corresponds to the ~1 mm hole.
- Push the wire in until the **insulation shoulder** bottoms against the connector - that auto-sets the crimp depth.
- Both hump locations (A and B) work, but **B (larger)** crimps the metal cylinder into a cleaner u-shape. Keep the connector's split seam at the middle of the curve so the metal cooperates as it folds.
- Pull-test the joint with one hand. If you need two hands to crimp, you have the wrong die or wrong tool.
- These terminals are designed for **one-time or few-times** connection - not many connect/disconnect cycles.
