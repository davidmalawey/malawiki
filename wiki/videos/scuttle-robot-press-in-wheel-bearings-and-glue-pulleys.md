## Overview

Two SCUTTLE assembly tutorials in one short video. First: a clever no-arbor-press technique for installing wheel bearings using SCUTTLE's own M8 bolt and wheel spacers as a pulling tool — finger-tight, no hammering, no damage. Second: how to glue the HTD5-pulley to the urethane tire with cyanoacrylate gel, including the critical detail that bonding to the urethane (rubber) is ~100x stronger than bonding to the inner nylon/HDPE plastic, so glue placement is everything.

## Key takeaways

- Press bearings in by sandwiching them between SCUTTLE's wheel spacers and tightening the M8 nut — the bolt acts as the pull-rod, the spacers act as the press jig.
- Hammering bearings in is a mistake; it can damage the races.
- The latest SCUTTLE wheel pulley (as of 2021-11) is a curved-tooth profile mating with HTD5 belt.
- Pulley teeth on the wheel-mating side are *datums*, not load paths — they just locate the pulley while glue cures.
- [[concepts/bonding-by-porosity|Cyanoacrylate]] grabs urethane far better than nylon/HDPE; place the bead so it lands on the urethane surface, not the inner black plastic ring.
- Use *gel* CA, not liquid — it stays where you put it.
- Verify a small "squish" of glue all around the perimeter as proof of full contact.
- Weight the joint, give it ~5 minutes before tug-testing.
- Belt alignment check after curing: the pulley should clear the wheel teeth without rubbing on either side.
