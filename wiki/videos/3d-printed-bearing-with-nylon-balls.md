## Overview

A short demo of a 3D-printed ABS bearing race running on nylon balls, with a Sharpie witness mark to show rotation and a drop of silicone-based synthetic grease added mid-test. The video is a working-placeholder prototype for the OpenSpin project — David's argument in the (later-added) description is that fully-documented, parametric mechanical parts matter more than being best-in-class.

## Key takeaways

- The bearing is intentionally not optimal — it is a "working placeholder" meant to anchor a documented digital backbone others can improve.
- Tolerances can be tightened on the next iteration and the bearing can be allowed to wear in for smaller running clearances.
- Synthetic [[entities/materials/silicone|silicone]]-based grease changes feel and sound noticeably.
- Core thesis: **accessibility for experts through documentation** — a documented mediocre design beats an undocumented great one.
