## Overview

A 69-second pitch video where David proposes hooking a vintage DC slot-car set up to [[concepts/m2m-iot-communication|MQTT machine-to-machine]] so it can count laps, track scores, and stream live — turning a cheap toy into a connected race track for [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] bots-on-bots demos.

## Key takeaways

- The slot-car runs on the same DC voltage as SCUTTLE, opening the door to shared power infrastructure.
- Hook the lap counter to MQTT, rack up scores, and consider a 24-hour live stream.
- Frames a teaser for upcoming videos on connecting hardware to the internet via [[concepts/m2m-iot-communication|machine-to-machine]] communication.
