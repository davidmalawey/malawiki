## Overview

Demonstrates installing brass M2.5 heat-set [[entities/tools/threaded-insert|threaded inserts]] (3.4 mm length, McMaster 94180A321) into the SCUTTLE battery box [[entities/materials/abs|ABS]] plastic parts using a 40 W soldering iron with the McMaster-supplied insert tip.

## Key takeaways

- The hole is sized to give a slight interference fit with the insert's outer knurls.
- Inserts have a **top and a bottom**: the leading (smaller-diameter) end goes in first, the larger-diameter top sits flush.
- **Don't force it.** When the iron is at full temperature, the weight of the iron plus slight pressure is enough; if you have to push, it isn't hot enough yet.
- The brass heats up in **3-4 seconds** once the iron is at full temperature.
- Press the insert in **just shy of flush**, let it sit a moment, then push it down a second time to compensate for the slight rebound as the plastic cools.
- The shoulder on the insert tip must clear the surrounding plastic so you don't melt the part around the hole.
- Pushing the insert just **beyond flush** ensures the mating part can clamp directly onto plastic, not bottom-out on the insert top.
- Same procedure works for [[entities/materials/acrylic|acrylic]] holes.
