## Overview

A short Texas A&M ETID lab support video. TA Aniket walks students through soldering lead wires to strain-gauge copper tabs for instrumentation labs 1 and 2. Procedure covers fixturing the wire with drafting tape, heating the iron to 650 F, confirming temperature by melting flux, and joining the wire to the copper tab with a clean fillet.

## Key takeaways

- Pre-fixture the lead wire on the copper tab with drafting tape so it stays put while soldering.
- Set the soldering station to 650 F and let it preheat about a minute.
- Verify the tip is hot enough by melting flux on it before touching the joint.
- Position the iron at the wire-to-copper-tab junction and feed flux to both surfaces simultaneously.
- A good joint is a clean visual fillet between wire, solder, and the copper tab — verify later by hooking up to a strain indicator and confirming readings.
- Show the work to your TA for visual inspection before proceeding.
