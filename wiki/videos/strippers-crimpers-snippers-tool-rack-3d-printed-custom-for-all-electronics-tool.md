## Overview

A 54-second timelapse of David designing and printing a custom 3D-printed wall rack to hold all the basic hand tools (strippers, crimpers, snippers, etc.) used to assemble SCUTTLE robots. After his bench overflowed with loose tools, the visual-access rack pays back the postponed effort. CAD is published on GrabCAD.

## Key takeaways

- Procrastinated projects often cost more in cumulative friction than the project takes to finish.
- Shadow-board / visible-rack thinking: if you can see all your tools, you reach for the right one faster.
- CAD is shared on [[entities/brands/grabcad|GrabCAD]] for anyone to print.
