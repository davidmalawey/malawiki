## Overview

A 47-second clip showing an early Payload Deck add-on for [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE robot]] — a stacking deck/bin platform that mounts on the rail and vertical column. Mostly a clearance check: confirming the bin sits below the rail, doesn't foul the vertical column, and that cables don't bind.

## Key takeaways

- The Payload Deck is a SCUTTLE add-on (Deck v1) that turns the platform into a carrier for bins or instrumented data-collection rigs.
- The mount uses the rail and a vertical column; clearance must be checked against both.
- Project also documented at scuttlerobot.org/2022/04/12/deck-v1/ and hackster.io/brey-caraway/deck-7198fe (referenced in description).

## Techniques demonstrated

- Hardware fit-check / interference check before final assembly.
- [[concepts/cable-management|Cable management]] verification ("oh crap these cables") as part of integration.
