## Overview

A 42-second post-mortem: [[entities/people/david-malawey|David]] used a clear two-part epoxy on roughed-up [[entities/materials/abs|ABS]], left it overnight, and the bond peeled cleanly off the printed surface with very little force. This is the original empirical test that motivated his later running series on which adhesive actually works on which substrate.

## Key takeaways

- Clear two-part epoxy did not bond to ABS, even with surface roughening.
- The peel left the printed lines visible cast into the cured epoxy - a perfect mold of the substrate, with zero adhesion.
- Conclusion: need to find another glue for ABS. (Later videos chase this down.)
