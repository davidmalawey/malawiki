## Overview

David builds a 12V splitter harness to feed both the motor driver and the [[entities/tools/beaglebone-blue|BeagleBone Blue]] from a single wall-wart power supply, before a battery pack is built. Demonstrates the proper crimping technique for 15A [[entities/tools/anderson-connector|Anderson Powerpole connectors]] using the smallest die in the [[entities/tools/anderson-crimper|Anderson crimper]] kit.

## Key takeaways

- Strip ~7 mm of insulation; use the stripper's adjustable stop to set repeatable length.
- Twist conductor strands before crimping so none escape the barrel.
- Tongue of the contact must face down in the front-most "15" die of the Powerpole crimper.
- Slide the crimped contact fully into the plastic housing until it clicks past the steel retention tab; tug-test to confirm.
- For the input pigtail (DC barrel side), tin stripped ends with solder so stray strands cannot bridge under the screw terminal.
