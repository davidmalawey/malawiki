## Overview

Step-by-step assembly of the SCUTTLE open-source payload deck — a cargo-bin carrier built from 3D-printed brackets, a laser-cut acrylic panel, and 30x30mm aluminum extrusion rails. Designed by Brey Caraway and shared on Hackster.io.

## Key takeaways

- The deck uses six pairs of printed blocks with a "twist" feature plus two end blocks holding 1.5mm hex set screws to clamp onto extrusions.
- Bin-capture brackets are oriented sweeping outward so a polyethylene cargo bin slides in easily.
- Tighten set screws to finger-snug only — full grip is overkill and can damage threads.
- Acrylic panel is strong but scratches easily; wipe the bin clean before sliding it in, and tape the panel during testing as a temporary scratch shield.
- Plastic-on-plastic (polyethylene against acrylic) is fine; the dirt embedded in the bin is what scratches.
