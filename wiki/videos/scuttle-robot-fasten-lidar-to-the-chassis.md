## Overview

Walkthrough of fastening the SICK TiM561 lidar to a [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] chassis using the provided black plastic bracket, M6 bolts, [[entities/tools/t-slot-nut|t-slot nuts]], and M3 screws into the lidar's threaded aluminum housing.

## Key takeaways

- M6 x 10 mm bolts plus t-slot nuts secure the bracket to the [[entities/tools/3030-extrusion|extrusion]] chassis.
- M3 x 10 mm screws pass through countersunk holes into threaded inserts in the lidar's aluminum body.
- If the plate is acrylic, countersinks must be machined manually; 3D printed plates can build them in.
- The bracket swivels — orient it so cables exit downward.
- USB micro is used for communication here, although the dedicated comm connector is more typical.
- Finger-tight only on the long end of the wrench — over-torquing cracks the plastic bracket.

## Tools and hardware used

- SICK TiM561 lidar
- M6 bolts, M3 screws
- [[entities/tools/t-slot-nut|t-slot nut]]
- [[entities/tools/3030-extrusion|3030 extrusion]] chassis
