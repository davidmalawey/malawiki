## Overview

A music-only animated walkthrough of the SCUTTLE Robot v3.0 mechanical assembly. The video sequences the rolling chassis, DIN-rail electronics tray, and wheel-and-belt subassemblies, intended to accompany the SCUTTLE Assembly Guide PDF at scuttlerobot.org. There is no spoken transcript — chapter markers are the navigation surface.

## Key takeaways

- Three main subassemblies: (1) rolling chassis with free-moving casters, (2) DIN rail with brackets for all electronics, (3) wheel assembly with motor, belt, and pulleys.
- Builders should pause-replay against the [Assembly Guide PDF](https://www.scuttlerobot.org/resource/guide/assembly-parts-guide/) for screw orientation and per-step detail.
- The chapter list is the spine: Overview > Corner Brackets > Casters > Chassis > Electronics > Wheel bracket > Combine All.
