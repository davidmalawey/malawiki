# Overview

A rolling synthesis of what this wiki covers. Updates with every ingest.

## The channel

David Malawey's YouTube channel. Craft / how-to / mechanical-engineering content aimed at hobbyists and students, especially those working in mechatronics, multidisciplinary engineering, and open-source robotics. David is formally a mechanical engineer — formative internship at [[entities/brands/toyota|Toyota]] in Georgetown, Kentucky, then engineer at the [[entities/places/toyota-design-center-saline|Toyota Design Center, Saline, Michigan]] — now teaching at [[entities/brands/texas-am|Texas A&M]] and shepherding the open-source [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] robot platform with international partners across Nigeria, Malaysia, Mexico, and Singapore-Johor.

His videos are deliberately built as **reference content you can jump around in**, not linear watches. Most are 20-70 minutes (some run to 75+) and catalog-shaped (20 tapes, 4 nut types, 10 cables cut open, 5 ways a ball helps an assembly, every actuator type in his lab, every adhesive he's tested). Engagement concentrates on videos that read like reference manuals.

## What's in the wiki

195 long-form videos ingested, ranging 23 seconds (a wheel-slip diagnostic clip) to 75 minutes, totaling ~57 hours of content. Earliest published 2018-10-30 (the novel-aquaponics 1st-year retrospective predating SCUTTLE) — the bulk of 2018-2019 era content was backfilled in batch 4. Bulk are 2022-2026 with batches 2-3 backfilling the foundational mechatronics era and 2019-2020 SCUTTLE origin, and batch 4 reaching back to the SCUTTLE bench-build kickoff and the pre-SCUTTLE aquaponics rig.

## Recurring themes

**"Borrow it instead of building it."** David's signature move is leveraging mass-produced precision — [[entities/tools/ball-bearings|ball bearings]], [[entities/materials/spring-steel|spring steel]], [[entities/materials/hdpe|HDPE]] tubing, [[entities/materials/paraffin-wax|paraffin wax]], EMT conduit, Unistrut, handy boxes — rather than machining or printing to spec. [[concepts/borrowing-tolerances|Borrowing tolerances]] is the central concept; [[concepts/free-data|free data]] from labels and manuals is the information-shaped version of the same idea. Variants: [[concepts/study-before-designing|study before designing]], [[concepts/benchmarking-design|benchmarking-driven design]], [[concepts/parts-ecosystem-design|parts-ecosystem design]], [[concepts/leverage-incumbent-engineering|leverage incumbent engineering]], [[concepts/vertical-non-integration|vertical non-integration]]. The [[entities/projects/openbox|OpenBox]] project is the most concrete embodiment — every accessory plugs into an off-the-shelf $2.50 stamped-steel handy box.

**Open-source design philosophy.** David publishes parametric source files (not just STLs), narratives, version provenance, and locally-sourced BOMs on [[entities/brands/grabcad|GrabCAD]], GitHub, and qr.net redirects. Bundled into a family of "Open*" projects: [[entities/projects/openlab|OpenLab]], [[entities/projects/openbox|OpenBox]], [[entities/projects/openjar|OpenJar]], [[entities/projects/openspin|OpenSpin]], [[entities/projects/openair|OpenAir]], [[entities/projects/openarm|OpenArm]], plus the [[entities/projects/open-me-project|Open ME Project]] (his entire mechanical-engineering bachelor's degree). [[concepts/open-source-hardware-publishing|Multi-tier publishing]] reaches the no-printer / has-printer / has-CAD / has-engineering / has-business audiences differently with the same release.

**SCUTTLE international expansion.** [[entities/projects/scuttle-robot|SCUTTLE]] is the spine. Active deployments: [[entities/projects/scuttle-nigeria|Nigeria]] (Dr. [[entities/people/gozie-nzebuka|Gozie Nzebuka]] at FUTO; filament extruder fundraiser), [[entities/projects/scuttle-malaysia|Malaysia]] (Dr. [[entities/people/dr-nurasmi|Nurasmi]] in Johor Bahru; Team Zamalik chili robot), Mexico tour.
